The studio

One designer, lots of dice.

Random Player Studio is an independent studio based in Rotterdam, born from a love of testing ideas fast.

We make simple mobile games and board games you can learn in five minutes and keep playing for years. Every game has its own channel on Discord, where we share prototypes, ask for feedback and look for testers.

01

Prototype fast

Ideas get playable in days, not months. If it isn't fun, we roll again.
