Project Shelter

Rescue them. Care for them. Find them a home.

Run a dog and cat shelter in first person. Rescue animals, feed, wash and heal them by hand, play fetch and tug-of-war, then find each one the right family. Working title.

Join the Discord
  • 4needs per animal: hunger, hygiene, exercise and affection
  • 20minutes per in-game day
  • 6languages
  • 1stperson: you care with your own hands
