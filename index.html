The studio

One designer, lots of dice.

Random Player Studio is an independent studio based in Rotterdam. It was born from a love of testing ideas fast: simple mobile games and board games you can learn in five minutes and keep playing for years.

We design in the open. Every game has its own channel on Discord, where we share prototypes, ask for feedback and look for testers.
